A Smaller, Slower Kind of Tool Site

CalcHarbor deliberately keeps its harbor small. Five calculators, each maintained like a boat that gets inspected before every voyage, beat a warehouse of half-working widgets. The pages load in a blink because there is nothing heavy aboard: no frameworks, no font downloads, no scripts phoning home. What you run here is plain HTML, CSS, and JavaScript served straight from the edge.

The philosophy shows up in small details. Results appear without page reloads. Labels say what they mean. Every formula is printed on the same page as the tool, with a worked example using ordinary numbers rather than abstract variables. If a method involves judgment calls, such as choosing a waste allowance or a tip rate, the page explains the trade-offs instead of hiding them behind a default.
